# Experimental Method

## WAAM fabrication

Three thin-wall builds (W1-W3) were fabricated using robotic gas metal arc wire-arc additive manufacturing. The setup used a Miller Phoenix 456 power source and a three-axis CNC motion system. ER70S-6 wire (1.2 mm diameter) was deposited on an ST37 substrate measuring 150 x 100 x 10 mm. A 98% Ar + 2% CO2 shielding mixture, a nominal 12 mm contact-tip-to-workpiece distance, a torch orientation normal to the substrate, and an interpass-temperature target of 165 °C were reported.

W1 used a two-pass/multi-bead strategy with approximately 30% overlap. W2 and W3 used single-pass layers. For this reason, the builds are treated as **combined deposition conditions**, rather than as a strictly one-factor heat-input experiment.

## Specimen extraction and testing

- **Metallography:** specimens from four wall-height zones; conventional grinding and polishing; 10% Nital; NEOPHOT 32 optical microscopy.
- **Grain size:** Heyn line-intercept method.
- **Hardness:** ASTM E92-17-based Vickers measurements at 10 kgf; five measurements in each of four zones.
- **Tensile:** ASTM E8-based sub-size specimens, reported thickness 1.5 mm, gauge length 9 mm, total length 28 mm; 0.5 mm/min on a SANTAM STM-20 machine; horizontal, vertical, and 45-degree extraction directions.
- **Impact:** ASTM E23-based Charpy specimens, 10 x 10 x 55 mm, four specimens per wall, room temperature.
- **Fractography:** VEGA3 TESCAN SEM examination of tensile and impact fracture surfaces.

## Public-release boundary

This repository exposes only curated summary metrics and representative images. Raw test-machine exports and orientation-resolved numerical results should be added only after specimen labels, units, and source files have been independently verified.
